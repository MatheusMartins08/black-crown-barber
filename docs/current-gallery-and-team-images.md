## Before prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. A single BEFORE photograph for a combined men's haircut and beard grooming slider on a barbershop website. Landscape 4:3. Fictional adult man around 30 sitting naturally in a classic leather barber chair, wearing a plain black barber cape. Eye-level three-quarter side angle facing slightly toward the right of frame, left cheek and temple visible, entire head and shoulders and recognizable chair headrest visible. Head centered with good headroom. Before grooming: moderately grown-out dark brown hair, approximately 8 cm on top and bulky 3 cm sides, untidy unstyled texture, fuzzy neckline and sideburns; hair DOES NOT reach shoulders. Moderately grown full beard approximately 2-3 cm long, uneven cheeks and neck contour, some stray hairs, but NOT an extremely long beard. Neutral expression, natural facial proportions, authentic skin pores, imperfect everyday texture, no beauty retouching. Soft natural window light in a real contemporary barbershop, warm wood cabinetry and mirrors softly out of focus. Photograph should look like an actual client awaiting a haircut, not a studio model or CGI. No other people, no hands, no text, no logos, no watermark, no collage. Later editing must preserve the exact face position, pose, chair, cape, background and lighting for pixel-aligned before/after comparison.

## After edit prompt

Reference: the generated before image.

Use case: identity-preserve edit. Edit this exact BEFORE photograph into the AFTER of a combined haircut and beard grooming comparison. Change ONLY scalp hair, beard and moustache together: beautiful professional men's haircut with a precise low-to-mid skin fade on sides and back, clean sideburn transition, shorter textured dark brown top about 4-5 cm styled neatly and naturally. Trim the beard into a handsome short full beard about 8-10 mm, uniform length, sharply defined cheek lines and clean neckline, groomed moustache with lips clear. Preserve the exact same face, head size and position, eyes, gaze, nose, ears, expression, original natural skin texture, seated pose, chair headrest, black cape, background cabinetry and mirrors, lighting, camera angle, framing, colors and image dimensions. Do not beautify skin or alter facial identity. Face and background must align pixel-for-pixel with the reference in an overlaid comparison slider. No camera move, no zoom, no text, no logos, no watermark, no collage. Photorealistic actual barber-client result; only hair and beard grooming should change.
